AssVrc test files

Synthetic test media for the AssVrc VRChat world (Phase 0.6 platform checks). No third-party content.

  • frame_counter.mp4: H.264 High 8-bit, 1920x1080, 23.976 fps, 120 s. Row 0 holds a 16-bit barcode of the frame number.
  • frame_counter_hevc10_2audio.mkv: same picture as HEVC Main 10 in MKV, with two audio tracks: ENG 440 Hz (default, low tone) and JPN 880 Hz (high tone).
  • frame_counter_store.zip: the matching .ass (Store zip). Row 1 holds the subtitle barcode.
  • big60.bin: 60 MiB of random bytes starting with PK 03 04 (string-loader size test).
